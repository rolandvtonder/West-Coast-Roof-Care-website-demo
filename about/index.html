Who we are

Leading the way inroof repair

West Coast Roof Care (Pty) Ltd is a roofing and waterproofing company based in Vredenburg. We protect homes, businesses and industrial properties throughout the West Coast and the Western Cape.

Our work covers roof repairs and leak detection, waterproofing, specialised roof coatings, re-roofing, gutter maintenance, damp proofing, high pressure cleaning, painting and asbestos encapsulation. Our waterproofing is backed by a 10-year waterproof warranty, and we are a Bostik approved applicator. We have extensive experience with the conditions coastal roofs face: salt air, wind, UV and heavy winter rain.

Most of our work comes from referrals. That is because we find the real cause of a problem, recommend the right system and finish what we start, on time.
