Our services

Complete propertyprotection

  • All types ofwaterproofing

    Torch-on, liquid, polyurethane and cementitious systems that stop water ingress, leaks and damp on roofs, balconies, basements and foundations.

    Learn more

  • Specialisedroof coating

    Protective coating systems such as RubberRoof, Metaltreat and Thermoproof that seal, rust-proof and extend the life of metal, tile and concrete roofs.

    Learn more

  • Roofreplacement

    Complete re-roofing for ageing, damaged and weathered roofs, from stripping and new trusses to new sheeting or tiles.

    Learn more

  • Roof repairs &leak detection

    Tracing leaks to their real source and repairing them properly: cracked sheets, slipped tiles, failed flashings, rusted fixings and storm damage, plus new roof installations.

    Learn more

  • High pressurecleaning

    Removes dirt, algae, moss and loose paint, and prepares roofs, walls and paving for coating, painting and waterproofing.

    Learn more

  • Gutter cleaning &maintenance

    Cleaning, repairing and sealing gutters, downpipes and box gutters so rainwater runs off the roof, not into the house.

    Learn more

  • Residential &industrial painting

    Interior and exterior painting for homes, commercial and industrial buildings, with coatings chosen for coastal conditions.

    Learn more

  • Damp proofing &wall sealing

    Treating rising and penetrating damp, repairing cracks and sealing walls so paint stops peeling and walls stay dry.

    Learn more

  • Asbestosencapsulation

    Registered asbestos contractor for the responsible encapsulation and removal of asbestos roofs and building materials, in line with the 2020 regulations.

    Learn more
